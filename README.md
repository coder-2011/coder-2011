## Things

**wrdn**  
An AI accountability system for your life. It uses ambient audio and visual context to build a long-term model of your goals, values, and habits, then helps you notice when you're drifting from what matters. I am building the stack from kernels to hardware.

**STRATUS UAV**  
A patent-pending autonomous VTOL aircraft for prescribed burning and wildfire mitigation. Built from scratch with a 3D-printed airframe, 15 km range, and 1.2 hour endurance, targeting a safer and lower-cost alternative to helicopter-based backburning.

**SBON**  
A wildfire prediction and prescribed-burn optimization system. It uses a tri-branch multimodal architecture over fire imagery, weather, and geography, then treats the predictor as an environment for reinforcement learning over burn placement.

**No Circles**  
A personalized daily newsletter that learns from what each reader responds to, builds a memory of the user, and creates issues designed to teach them about adjacent fields.

**PHOENIX UAV**  
Early wildfire detection UAV project. Placed 2nd at the California State Science Fair, 1st at regionals, won the Junior Lemelson-MIT Inventor Award, and earned selection for national competition.
